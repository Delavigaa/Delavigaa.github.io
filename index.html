AI media systems — production

IPTV monitoring & Arabic voice platform

An end-to-end platform that watches live multilingual broadcasts, transcribes them as they air, flags what matters, and speaks the result back in Arabic. Deployed for government use.

  • Ingests live multilingual broadcasts and transcribes in real time with OpenAI Whisper, processing video, audio, and captions through an FFmpeg, Node.js, and Nginx pipeline.
  • Surveillance intelligence layer with configurable keyword watchlists, automated evidence collection, transcript indexing, search, and alerting.
  • Automatic translation and Arabic text-to-speech for non-Arabic broadcasts.
  • Monitoring, logging, retries, and self-recovery so the pipeline keeps running unattended.
